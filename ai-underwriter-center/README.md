# AutoDeFi DAO AI Underwriter Center

Complete dark fintech/Web3 front-end for the AutoDeFi decentralized auto loan pool underwriting workflow.

## Included tabs

- Dashboard
- Applications Queue
- Risk Models
- Identity & KYC
- Income Verification
- Bank Analysis
- Vehicle Valuation
- Fraud Signals
- Conditional Approvals
- Funding Readiness
- Analytics
- Settings

## AutoDeFi alignment

This interface follows the AutoDeFi loan pool model:

- Tier 1: ADF staked collateral or one-third-plus down payment
- Tier 2: good credit with token/down-payment support
- Tier 3: good credit with no money down
- Tier 4: last-chance dealer lead flow with internal bank review for debt-to-service
- Approved deals are funded in full to dealers
- Borrowers repay through regional stable-value rails
- ADF remains staking, collateral, rewards, access, and governance utility
- ZONYCS recovery auction integration is referenced in settings and collateral workflows

```bash
npm install

```
