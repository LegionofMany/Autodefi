# ADF Utility

ADF is the AutoDeFi ecosystem token.

## Locked utility

ADF is used for:

- Staking
- Collateral support
- Platform access
- Rewards
- Governance
- Lender and dealer participation incentives
- Risk-tier participation and reputation support

## Locked non-use

ADF is not the default borrower loan repayment currency in V1. Borrowers should repay through regional stable-value rails unless the owner explicitly changes this rule.

## Product alignment

The UI may show ADF staking power, ADF rewards, governance power, treasury allocation, and collateral utility. It should not present ADF as the primary loan payment currency.
