# AutoDeFi Institutional Portal Positioning

AutoDeFi is a standalone institutional window portal connecting blockchain LP capital to dealership finance workflow.

## Inspiration Map

- Dealertrack: dealership workflow
- Bloomberg Terminal: command-center density
- Coinbase Institutional: custody and settlement posture
- Tesla Finance: clean digital borrower finance UX
- Copart: liquidation and auction logic
- Palantir: risk intelligence console
- MakerDAO: pooled capital, collateral and governance logic

## Positioning Statement

Blockchain LP capital to dealership funding infrastructure.
