# AutoDeFi DAO Dealer Marketplace Network

Front-end React/Vite dashboard for the AutoDeFi DeFi auto loan pool dealer marketplace.

## Included tabs
Dashboard, Marketplace, All Listings, Auctions, My Listings, Watchlist, Deals, Inventory Sold, Purchase Orders, Transfers, Floorplan Financing, Credit Lines, Funding History, Dealers, Dealer Directory, Ratings & Reviews, Performance, Analytics, Reports, Profile & Store, Users & Permissions, Integrations, Settings.

## AutoDeFi alignment
- Verified dealer-to-dealer marketplace network.
- Dealers can list, buy, auction, finance, and transfer inventory.
- ZONYCS-style marketplace and auction flows are represented.
- Approved borrower deals fund the dealer in full.
- Borrowers repay through regional stable-value rails.
- ADF remains utility for staking, collateral, access, rewards, governance, and ecosystem permissions.
- Interest yield flows to matching risk-tier LP/staker pools.
- Dealer verification, KYC, ratings, audit logs, escrow readiness, and risk controls are preserved visually.

